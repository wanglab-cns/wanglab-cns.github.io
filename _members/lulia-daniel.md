Lulia Daniel completed her studies in the Honours Life Science Co-op Program with a Minor in Biochemistry at McMaster University, where she developed a strong interest in cancer genetics through her co-op experiences in translational and clinical research. 
She is currently a Clinical Study & Regulatory Assistant with the Wang Lab at Princess Margaret Cancer Centre. In this role, she supports the coordination and regulatory management of ongoing clinical trials and study operations. Through her work, she supports advancing precision oncology research and clinical care.
