Xin (Kevin) Wang, MD, PhD, FRCPC, is a Medical Oncologist and Clinician Investigator at the Princess Margaret Cancer Centre, University Health Network, and Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto. His translational research program focuses on developing precision therapeutic and biomarker strategies for primary brain tumours. His laboratory integrates multi-omic datasets, computational oncology with patient samples and investigator-initiated clinical trials. A major focus is understanding the glioma tumour microenvironment and mechanisms of treatment resistance to develop novel immunotherapies and predictive biomarkers.
