# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Projects

Our research is focused on three main areas:

1. **Biomarker Discovery**: Developing minimally invasive and multidimensional biomarkers for precisison oncology.
2. **Target Discovery**: Developing a neurodevelopmental atlas for rational target discovery and drug repurposing in glioma.
3. **Clinical Trials**: Translating glioma cell states into new therapeutic strategies for glioma.


{% include section.html %}
# Biomarker Discovery
Biomarker discovery is a critical aspect of precision oncology, enabling the identification of molecular signatures that can guide treatment decisions and predict patient outcomes. Our research focuses on developing minimally invasive and multidimensional biomarkers that can inform clinical decision-making and improve patient care in glioma.

{% include section.html%}
# Target Discovery
Target discovery is essential for identifying new therapeutic opportunities in glioma. Our team is developing a comprehensive neurodevelopmental atlas that integrates multi-omics data to facilitate rational target discovery and drug repurposing, ultimately aiming to improve treatment options for patients with glioma.

{% include section.html %}
# Clinical Trials
Clinical trials are the cornerstone of translational research, bridging the gap between laboratory discoveries and patient care. Our team is dedicated to designing and conducting clinical trials that evaluate novel therapeutic strategies for glioma, with a focus on translating insights from glioma cell states into effective treatments.
